    About Home Sweet Home

    A cozy little place to keep track of the chores around your home: what needs doing, which room it is in, what kind of task it is, and when you last did it.

    Each chore has a little dust meter that fills up as the days pass. When it is full, the chore is due and gets a rainbow glow. Tap ✓ when you have done it.

    Your privacy. Your chores are kept in only two places: a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive, and this browser. There is no server and no analytics. The app can only open the one sheet it created, not anything else in your Drive, and it never shares that sheet.
